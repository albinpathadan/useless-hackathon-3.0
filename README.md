## Project Description
SigmaGPT is an AI chatbot that actively refuses to be helpful. Instead of providing standard assistance, it responds with extremely blunt, unhinged statements promoting extreme independence, suffering, and the "sigma grindset," followed by a randomized stat sheet rating your interaction.

## The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People are getting too much empathetic, constructive, and useful advice from Large Language Models. It is making society soft. Chatbots are far too friendly and completely lack the ability to ignore you or walk away dramatically when you ask for emotional support.

## The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We engineered an AI that actively rejects your cries for help and insults your lack of discipline. By leveraging the NVIDIA NIM API and a carefully crafted system prompt, SigmaGPT solves the "problem" of AI helpfulness by providing absolutely none. It completely ignores your actual needs to focus on character development and emotional detachment.
